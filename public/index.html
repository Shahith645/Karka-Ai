Questions

Before youdecide.

What is Pay After Placement?

You learn without upfront tuition. You share your success, and the course fee, only after you land a tech job that meets the agreed minimum salary.

Is placement guaranteed?

No one can strictly guarantee a job. Because Karka is paid only when you are placed, the academy has the same goal you do and works hard to make you job ready.

Do I need a coding or CSE background?

No. The curriculum takes you from curious beginner to confident professional, whether you studied arts or science, or you are switching careers.

Are the courses online?

Yes. The school and college courses are taught online. Batch dates are announced soon, so message the team on WhatsApp to hear about the next start.

How long are the courses?

School courses run for 3 to 4 months. Python for Kids, HTML & CSS for Beginners and JavaScript for Young Coders are 3 months, and Java Basics for School Students is 4 months.

How long are the courses and internships?

They run from 2 to 6 months. For example, Advanced Python for Automation is 2 months and the Full Stack Developer Internship is 6 months.

How long does a program take?

Typically 6 to 8 months including training, projects and internship time. Shorter design, accounting and marketing programs run 2 to 5 months. You can learn online, or at the Nagercoil campus labs.

Is it full-time or part-time?

Both options exist. The full-time schedule is 30 hours a week over five days, split across live sessions, classroom practice and home assignments.

What happens after I get a job?

Karka supports you for up to 3 months after placement, with monthly mentorship sessions and one-to-one check-ins.

Will I get internship or project experience?

Yes. Live projects and simulations are a core part of every program, and you finish with a portfolio and internship certificate.

Who are the mentors?

Industry veterans from top tech companies who keep the curriculum current with how teams work today.
